**Quick answer:** The best gifts for a Seattle music lover support the local scene: **vinyl from an independent record store** (Easy Street in West Seattle, Sonic Boom in Ballard, Silver Platters in SoDo, Daybreak in Fremont), **tickets to a local show**, **music lessons** or **live music for their next party**.

It's Black Friday. Skip the mall and spend your gift money where the music actually happens.

## 1. Records from a local shop

Seattle still has great independent record stores. A few to start with:

- **Easy Street Records**, West Seattle: a record store and cafe that's a local landmark
- **Sonic Boom Records**, Ballard: an indie shop with in-store events
- **Silver Platters**, SoDo: a longtime Seattle music store
- **Daybreak Records**, Fremont: a neighborhood record shop

Not sure what they own? A **gift card** saves you from buying their third copy of *Nevermind*.

## 2. Tickets to a local show

Concert tickets beat another sweater. Small-venue shows are cheap and close-up, and they're a great night out together. Our next one is **Second Serving Saturday at Jules Maes Saloon, Nov 28**, with tickets at $10 in advance. [Get tickets](https://tickets.venuepilot.com/e/spotted-zebra-pink-182-lark-v-owl-2026-11-28-jules-maes-seattle-acaacf).

## 3. Lessons

For the friend who's been "about to learn guitar" for ten years, a few lessons with a local teacher are the push they need. Many Seattle music shops and schools sell lesson packages as gifts.

## 4. Gear upgrades for the musician

- **A good set of strings, picks or drumsticks.** Musicians always need more.
- **A quality instrument cable**, the thing everyone forgets until it crackles mid-song
- **Earplugs made for musicians**, which protect hearing without muffling the music

## 5. A live band for their next party

The big one: give them a band for their birthday, anniversary or next backyard party. It's an experience gift the whole guest list gets to enjoy. Private parties [start around $400](/blog/how-much-does-a-cover-band-cost-in-seattle/). [Ask about a date](/#book).
