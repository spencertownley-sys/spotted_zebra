Our story

Five people.
One very unusual zebra.

Spotted Zebra started in late 2021 but didn’t find its current form until early 2024. Since then, SZ has played bars and parties all around the greater Seattle area.

From 80s hits and 90s rock to 2000s alternative and modern bangers, SZ has a song for anyone and everyone. If the dance floor is empty, we take it personally.
