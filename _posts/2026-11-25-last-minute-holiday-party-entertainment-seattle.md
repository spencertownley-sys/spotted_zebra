Be flexible on the **night**, send the band your **date, venue, guest count and hours** in your first message, and have a **sound plan** ready. **Spotted Zebra has Friday and Saturday nights open in December**, apart from the Christmas and New Year's holidays.

It's late November, the party's on the calendar and the entertainment line on the planning doc is still blank. Don't panic. Here's how to fill it fast.

## Why it's not too late

December weekends are busy, but bands don't book every night. Small and mid-sized bands often have openings into mid-December, especially for private parties and company events.

## How to book fast

1. **Send everything in one message:** date, start and end time, venue or address, guest count and the kind of party. That's what gets you a quote the same day.
2. **Be flexible on the night.** If Saturday is taken, a Friday or a weeknight might be wide open.
3. **Know your sound situation.** Does the venue have a sound system? If not, the band may need to bring one.
4. **Be ready to pay a deposit.** It holds your date. Spotted Zebra takes 25%.

Our [Gig Builder](/#book) asks for exactly these details, so you can send it all in a couple of minutes.

## What a band needs from you on short notice

- **Space:** about 12 × 16 ft for a five-piece band
- **Power:** a couple of outlets on separate circuits
- **A loading spot** close to the stage
- **A timeline:** when guests arrive, when dinner and toasts happen, and when people will want to dance

## Spotted Zebra's December availability

We're open **most Friday and Saturday nights in December**, except around Christmas and New Year's. That covers company parties, house parties, bar nights and benefits. [Check your date](/#book).
