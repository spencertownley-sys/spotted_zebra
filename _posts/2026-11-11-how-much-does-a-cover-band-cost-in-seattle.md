**Quick answer:** In the Seattle area, hiring a live cover band typically **starts around $400 for a private party** and **around $750 for a corporate event**. The price goes up with **the date, how long you want music, travel and whether the band has to bring its own sound system**. Most bands ask for a **deposit** to hold your date.

We get asked this more than anything else, so here's a straight answer, including what pushes the price up or down.

## Typical cover band prices in Seattle

| Event type | Typical starting point | What's usually included |
|---|---|---|
| Private party (backyard, birthday, house party) | From about $400 | Up to three 40-minute sets |
| Corporate or holiday party | From about $750 | Up to three 40-minute sets, clean set list |
| Bar or venue gig | Varies | Usually a door deal or flat fee set by the venue |

These are starting points, not final prices. Your quote depends on the details below.

## What changes the price?

**1. The date.** Friday and Saturday nights in summer and December are in the highest demand.

**2. How long you want music.** Most quotes cover a set amount of playing time. Ask what it costs to add a set.

**3. Number of musicians.** A five-piece band is five people's night of work, plus rehearsal, gear and travel. Duos and trios cost less but sound smaller.

**4. Sound system.** Many venues have one the band can plug into. If yours doesn't, the band brings its own, which can add to the quote. Spotted Zebra can bring a sound system but prefers to use the venue's.

**5. Travel.** Gigs in Seattle, Bellevue and the surrounding area are standard. Farther trips usually add travel costs.

**6. Special requests.** Learning songs that aren't on the band's list takes rehearsal time.

## Do bands take a deposit?

Most do. It holds your date so the band can turn down other offers. Spotted Zebra takes a **25% deposit** to lock in a date, with the rest due closer to the event.

## How to get an accurate quote

Tell the band:

- your **date** and **city or venue**
- **how many guests** you expect
- **how long** you want music
- whether the venue has a **sound system**
- any **must-play songs**
