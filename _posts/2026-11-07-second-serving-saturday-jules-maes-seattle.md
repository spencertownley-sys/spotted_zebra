## About Jules Maes Saloon

Jules Maes sits on Airport Way South in Georgetown, Seattle's old industrial neighborhood that's now packed with bars, breweries and art spaces. It's one of Seattle's oldest bars, dating to the late 1800s, and it's a proper saloon with a stage and the kind of room where a good band can fill every corner.
