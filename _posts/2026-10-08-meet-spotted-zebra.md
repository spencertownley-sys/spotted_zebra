If you've ended up here, there's a decent chance you've seen us at a bar or a backyard party somewhere around Seattle and wondered who those people with the zebra logo were. Hi. We're **Spotted Zebra**.

## How we got here

Spotted Zebra started in late 2021, and like most bands, it took a few rounds to get the lineup right. The five of us found our current form in early 2024, and we've been playing bars and parties around the greater Seattle area ever since.

## What we play

Our whole idea is simple: **play the songs people actually want to hear.** That means:

- **80s hits** that get the whole room singing the chorus
- **90s rock** for everyone who still knows every word
- **2000s alternative**, the stuff you blasted in the car with the windows down
- **Modern bangers**, so the younger crowd doesn't feel left out

We'd rather see a packed dance floor than play something obscure to impress other musicians. If you've got a request, tell us. Our [jukebox](/#jukebox) is a good place to start.

## Where to find us

Keep an eye on the [shows section](/#shows) and follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/spottedzebramusic) for upcoming dates. Want us at your bar, party or event? [Build your gig](/#book) and we'll get back to you.

See you on the dance floor.
