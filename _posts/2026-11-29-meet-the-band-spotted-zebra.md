**Quick answer:** Spotted Zebra is a five-piece Seattle cover band: **Vicki** and **Ann** on vocals, **Brian** on guitar, **Spencer** on bass and backing vocals, and **Brent** on drums. The band started in 2021 and found its current lineup in 2024.

You've seen the zebra logo. You've maybe danced to us at a bar in Georgetown or a backyard somewhere around Seattle. Here are the people actually making the noise.

## Vicki: vocals

Vicki keeps the band in line at practice. When five people all have opinions about the bridge in "Zombie," someone has to get us back to work, and that someone is Vicki.

## Ann: vocals

Ann and Vicki have been best friends since high school, which explains the harmonies, and the inside jokes nobody else in the band understands.

## Brian: guitar

Brian hosts our practice space, so in a very real sense none of this happens without Brian. Before Spotted Zebra, Brian played in a band called **Free Beer**, which is still the best band name any of us has ever heard.

## Spencer: bass and backing vocals

Spencer is the youngest member of the band, runs our social media and makes our show posters. If you've seen a Spotted Zebra flyer, Spencer made it.

## Brent: drums

Brent plays drums for us and guitar in another band, because one instrument wasn't enough.

## How it all fits together

We started in 2021, went through a few lineups like every band does, and became the five of us in 2024. Since then we've played bars and private parties around the greater Seattle area, including Jules Maes Saloon, the Shanghai Room, the Orient Express, Retro Emporium and The Sandbar.

What we play: classic rock, 80s hits, 90s grunge, 2000s alternative and modern bangers. Here's [every song on the list](/jukebox/). If the dance floor is empty, we take it personally.
