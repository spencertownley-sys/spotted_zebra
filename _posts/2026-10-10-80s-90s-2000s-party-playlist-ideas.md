Every party has a sweet spot: the decade where everyone in the room suddenly knows every word. Find it and the dance floor takes care of itself. Here's how we think about it.

## Start with the crowd

The easiest rule: **people love the music from when they were 15 to 25.** Look at the age range of your guests and you'll know where the singalongs are hiding.

- **Mostly 40s and 50s?** Lean into 80s hits and 90s rock.
- **Mostly 30s?** The 2000s alternative years are your secret weapon.
- **Mixed ages, like a wedding or a company party?** Mix it up. More on that below.

## Why mixing decades works

A set that jumps from an 80s anthem to a 2000s singalong to something from this year keeps every group on its feet. Each song is somebody's favorite, and nobody sits out for long.

## Think about the shape of the night

- **Early:** familiar, mid-tempo songs while people grab a drink and warm up
- **Peak:** the biggest, loudest, most singable songs
- **Late:** a couple of crowd-pleasers to finish on a high, plus that one song everyone screams the words to

## Let your guests vote

Not sure what your crowd wants? Ask them. Send people to our [jukebox](/#jukebox) before the party, have them tick their favorites, and send the requests our way.

## The short version

Pick the decade your crowd grew up with, mix in a little from every era, and save the biggest songs for when the room is full. Or leave the setlist to us. [Tell us about your event](/#book).
