    About the band

    Five people.
    One party.

    Spotted Zebra is a five-piece cover band from Seattle. We started in 2021, found our current lineup in 2024, and have been playing bars and parties all around the greater Seattle area ever since.

    We play songs for anyone and everyone, from classic rock, to 80s hits, 90s grunge, 2000s alternative, and modern bangers. If the dance floor is empty, we take it personally.

    Good questions

    Hiring a Seattle cover band? Start here.

    What kind of band is Spotted Zebra?

    A five-piece cover band from Seattle. We play the songs people actually want to hear: 80s hits, 90s rock, 2000s alternative and today’s bangers, plus a few originals of our own.

    Where do you play?

    Bars, private parties and events around the greater Seattle area and the Pacific Northwest. Planning something further away? Tell us about it.

    What kinds of events do you play?

    Bar and venue gigs, private parties, weddings, corporate events and festivals. If there’s a dance floor (or there should be), we’re interested.

    Can we request songs?

    Yes. List your must-plays in the Gig Builder when you book and we’ll work them into the night.

    How do we book Spotted Zebra?

    Fill in the Gig Builder below and it lands straight in our inbox, or email contact@spottedzebra.party. Tell us the date, the venue and roughly how many people are coming.
