**Quick answer:** Thanksgiving weekend in Seattle runs **Thursday, November 26 to Sunday, November 29, 2026**. Highlights: the free **downtown tree lighting at Westlake Park on Friday, Nov 27** (tree and star lit at 5 PM, then fireworks), the **holiday carousel** at Westlake Park, the **Seattle Christmas Market** at Seattle Center, and live music on **Saturday night**, including Spotted Zebra at [Second Serving Saturday](/blog/second-serving-saturday-jules-maes-seattle/) in Georgetown.

Everyone's back in town, the leftovers are piling up, and you need a reason to get out of the house. Here's a day-by-day plan.

## Thursday, Nov 26: Thanksgiving Day

Most of the city is closed, so this is the day for family, food and football. If you're hosting, it's also a good day to line up the rest of the weekend.

## Friday, Nov 27: Tree lighting downtown

Downtown Seattle's **Tree Lighting Celebration** is the day after Thanksgiving at **Westlake Park**. Activities start during the day, the tree and star light up at **5 PM**, and fireworks follow. It's free and it gets crowded, so get there early. The **Westlake Park holiday carousel** opens the same day and runs into early January.

## Saturday, Nov 28: Live music in Georgetown

Walk off the pie at **Jules Maes Saloon** for **Second Serving Saturday**:

- **8 PM:** Pink-182 (Blink-182 tribute)
- **9 PM:** Spotted Zebra
- **10 PM:** Lark vs Owl

Doors at 7 PM, 21+. Tickets are $10 in advance or $15 at the door. [Get tickets](https://tickets.venuepilot.com/e/spotted-zebra-pink-182-lark-v-owl-2026-11-28-jules-maes-seattle-acaacf). If you're making a night of it, here's our guide to [live music in Georgetown](/blog/live-music-in-georgetown-seattle/).

## Sunday, Nov 29: Holiday market and a slow day

The **Seattle Christmas Market** at Seattle Center, next to the Space Needle, runs from late November through late December, so Sunday is a good time to wander it. The **Seattle Marathon** is also that weekend, so expect some road closures.

## Tips for the weekend

- **Check times before you go.** Holiday event details change. Look at each event's official site the week of.
- **Take transit downtown on Friday.** The tree lighting draws a big crowd, and parking is tight.
- **Buy show tickets in advance.** They're cheaper and you're guaranteed to get in.

## FAQ

**When is the Seattle tree lighting in 2026?**
Friday, November 27, 2026, at Westlake Park. The tree is lit at 5 PM, followed by fireworks.

**What is there to do in Seattle the Saturday after Thanksgiving?**
Holiday shopping and markets during the day, and live music at night, like Second Serving Saturday at Jules Maes Saloon in Georgetown.

**Is the Seattle Christmas Market open Thanksgiving weekend?**
It runs from late November through late December. It's closed on Thanksgiving Day, so plan for Friday through Sunday.
