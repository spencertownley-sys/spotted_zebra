Bio

Spotted Zebra is a five-piece cover band from Seattle. We started in 2021, found our current lineup in 2024, and have been playing bars and parties all around the greater Seattle area ever since.

We play songs for anyone and everyone, from classic rock, to 80s hits, 90s grunge, 2000s alternative, and modern bangers. If the dance floor is empty, we take it personally.
