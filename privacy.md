## Who is responsible

Spotted Zebra (https://spottedzebra.party) is run by [PLACEHOLDER: owner legal name], Seattle, Washington, United States. Contact: [contact@spottedzebra.party](mailto:contact@spottedzebra.party).

## What is collected and why

- **Booking form (Gig Builder):** your name, email, event type, date, crowd size, decades wanted and a free-text message. We use it to reply to your booking request.
- **Mailing list:** your email address and the page you signed up from. We use it to send news of new shows and ticket links.
- **Emails you send to the band address:** these are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the band's Gmail inbox.
- **Technical data:** like every website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure.

Legal bases under EU and UK law: providing the service you asked for (contract), keeping the site secure and working (legitimate interests), and your consent where this policy says something is optional.

## How the forms work

Both forms are sent by FormSubmit, which delivers each submission by email to the band's inbox (Gmail, through Cloudflare Email Routing). Nothing you type into either form is stored anywhere else on this website.

## The mailing list and how to unsubscribe

Signing up means you agree to get an occasional email about new shows and ticket links. To unsubscribe at any time, reply "unsubscribe" to any email from us or email [contact@spottedzebra.party](mailto:contact@spottedzebra.party), and we will remove your address. Your address is not sold or shared with anyone else for their own use.

## Cookies and storage {#storage}

This site does not set any cookies and does not use advertising or cross-site tracking. It only uses the browser storage it needs to work.

| Key | Where | Purpose | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sz-popup-&lt;show date&gt; | sessionStorage | Remembers that you already saw the next-show notice during this visit | Necessary |
| cookie-consent | localStorage | Remembers that you have seen the cookie notice | Necessary |

You can clear or block site storage in your browser settings. Use the "Cookie settings" button in the page footer to see the notice again.
