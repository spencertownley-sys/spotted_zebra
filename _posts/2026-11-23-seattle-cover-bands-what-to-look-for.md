**Quick answer:** Before you book a Seattle cover band, check **five things**: a **song list** that matches your crowd, **real live video** (not just a highlight reel), whether they **bring sound** or need yours, **how long they play** (sets and breaks), and **clear pricing and deposit terms**. A good band will answer all five in one email.

There are a lot of cover bands in the Seattle area, from duos in brewery corners to ten-piece horn bands. Here's how to find the right one for your night, whether you end up booking us or someone else.

## 1. Does their song list match your crowd?

Ask for the full song list, not just "we play everything." Then picture your guests. A crowd in their 40s and 50s will light up for 80s and 90s hits. A mixed-age party needs songs from every decade.

Our list is public: [every song Spotted Zebra plays](/jukebox/), from classic rock to this year's hits.

## 2. Can you see them actually play?

Highlight reels are edited to look great. Ask for:

- **full-song live video**, ideally from a real gig
- a **public show** you can drop into

You can [watch our videos](/videos/) or [catch an upcoming show](/#shows).

## 3. Do they bring sound?

Some bands bring a full sound system. Others need the venue's. Ask up front, because it affects your venue choice and your price. Spotted Zebra can bring sound but prefers to use a venue's system when one is available.

## 4. How long do they play?

Most cover bands play in sets with short breaks. Ask how many sets, how long each one runs and what happens during breaks. Spotted Zebra plays **up to three 40-minute sets**.

## 5. Is the pricing clear?

You should get a clear quote and clear deposit terms in writing. In the Seattle area, private parties typically start around $400 and corporate events around $750. See our [cover band pricing guide](/blog/how-much-does-a-cover-band-cost-in-seattle/). Spotted Zebra takes a 25% deposit to hold a date.

## Bonus: do they feel like a fit?

The best cover bands care whether the dance floor is full, not just whether they played the songs. Ask how they read a room, and whether they take requests. (We do.)

## Questions to copy into your first email

1. Can you send your song list?
2. Do you have full-song live video?
3. Do you bring a sound system?
4. How many sets, and how long?
5. What's the price for my date and hours, and what's the deposit?
6. Can we request a song or two?

## FAQ

**How do I find a good cover band in Seattle?**
Look for a public song list, full-song live video, clear pricing and a band that plays public shows you can see first.

**How far ahead should I book?**
For December or summer weekends, 6–10 weeks. Other times, 3–4 weeks is often enough.

**Do Seattle cover bands take requests?**
Many do. Spotted Zebra takes requests when you book and can learn new songs.
